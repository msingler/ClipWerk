1. What ClipWerk is

ClipWerk is a private desktop application that runs locally on the developer's own Windows computer. It is used exclusively by its developer to prepare short videos and upload them as drafts to the developer's own TikTok account via the official TikTok Content Posting API. ClipWerk is not sold, distributed or offered as a service to third parties.

2. Use of TikTok

ClipWerk only acts on the TikTok account that its user has explicitly authorized through TikTok Login Kit. Videos are sent to the TikTok inbox as drafts; publishing is always done manually by the account owner inside the TikTok app. Use of TikTok remains subject to the TikTok Terms of Service and Community Guidelines.

3. Content

The user is solely responsible for the content prepared and published with ClipWerk and for holding the necessary rights or permissions (for example through creator clipping programs) for any third-party material.

4. No warranty

ClipWerk is provided "as is", without warranty of any kind. The developer is not liable for any damages arising from its use, to the extent permitted by law.

5. Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time by disconnecting ClipWerk in the application settings or by removing the app under TikTok → Settings → Security → Apps and services.
